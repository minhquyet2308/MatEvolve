### 1) STRUCTURE OVERVIEW
Formula: NdSmFe12Co5
Structure Type: Th2Zn17-type (Rhombohedral 2:17)
Space Group: R3
Composition: 19 atoms per unit cell (Z=1), representing a rare-earth to transition-metal ratio of 2:17 with mixed Nd/Sm and Fe/Co ordering.
Lattice Note: Rhombohedral primitive cell (a=6.38 Å, alpha=82.6°) which corresponds to the standard rhombohedral 2:17 phase; the angle indicates a slightly compressed c-axis in the hexagonal setting.
Effective Symmetry: Physical symmetry is R-3m (No. 166), though represented in P1 to specify the distinct ordering of Nd/Sm on the 6c sites and Co on the 6c/9d sites.

### 3) HARD MAGNET ASSESSMENT
Anisotropy: weak – The rhombohedral 2:17 structure with this Fe/Co ratio typically exhibits easy-plane anisotropy; Nd and Sm ions in these sites do not provide uniaxiality without interstitial modification.
Exchange: FM – Strong ferromagnetic coupling is driven by direct 3d-3d exchange, significantly enhanced by Co substitution at the dumbbell and 9d sites.
Stability: STABLE – The Th2Zn17-type structure is a robust equilibrium phase for RE-TM systems with a 2:17 stoichiometry.
Verdict: soft – While possessing high magnetization and Curie temperature, the easy-plane anisotropy makes it a soft magnet unsuitable for permanent magnet applications without nitriding or carbonation.
