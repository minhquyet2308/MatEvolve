### 1) STRUCTURE OVERVIEW
Formula: PrFe10B
Structure Type: Vacancy-ordered Nd2Fe14B-derivative

Lattice Note: Tetragonal lattice (a=b=8.754 Å, c=11.862 Å) with an elongated c-axis (c/a ≈ 1.35), characteristic of RE-TM-B hard magnets.
Effective Symmetry: Physically tetragonal (likely P42/mnm or I4/mcm) despite the P1 representation, with atoms organized in layers along the [001] direction.

### 3) HARD MAGNET ASSESSMENT
Anisotropy: strong – Pr ions in a non-cubic coordination cage provide high magnetocrystalline anisotropy via CEF interactions.
Exchange: FM – Fe-Fe bond lengths (2.5-2.7 Å) are optimized for strong ferromagnetic direct exchange, avoiding AFM frustration.
Stability: STABLE – The B-Fe6 trigonal prisms provide a robust chemical scaffold that stabilizes the 1:10:1 stoichiometry.
Verdict: Hard magnet – The combination of Pr-driven anisotropy and a dense, FM-coupled Fe framework makes this a high-performance permanent magnet candidate.
